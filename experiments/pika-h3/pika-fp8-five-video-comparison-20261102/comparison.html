What was tested

Precision and execution

  • Base: unchanged minimax_h3_fl2va_pruned_int8_convrot.safetensors, MiniMax revision 0bd506d2.
  • Pika: official Comfy BF16 FL2VA adapter, revision 3ff16644, SHA-256 verified. Converted on load to FP8 E4M3, per-tensor absmax scales. Activations are dynamically scaled to FP8 before both low-rank projections.
  • Real FP8 GEMMs: explicit torch._scaled_mm with FP8 operands. No BF16 fallback path exists. Bypass outputs return to the original BF16 activation type; the fused fc2 merge uses FP8 operands with FP32 output, retaining the existing base merge route.
  • Everything else: strength 1, SageAttention 2.2.0, dynamic VRAM, Pika re-noise sampler, 3 steps, shifts 12/3, seed 427739230855701667. Same prompt/image/last image/driving audio. No TeaCache.
  • Hardware: one RTX 5090 on gpu5090-79. Fresh processes; no competing GPU processes observed at the pre-run gates. Driver-only idle baseline: 2 MiB.

Measurement and interpretation

  • Selection: Red Fox, Bengal Tiger, African Elephant, Scarlet Macaw and Barn Owl were selected before generation to span fur, skin, feathers, gestures and landscape/portrait.
  • Video: 15.0833 seconds, 362 frames, 24 FPS; four 1280×720 outputs and one 720×1280 output. Native landscape generation is 1280×736, cropped by the unchanged production workflow.
  • Metrics: FFmpeg PSNR and SSIM on decoded YUV420P, corresponding frames with timestamps reset. No resizing, motion alignment, skipped frames or cherry-picked segments. PSNR uses combined MSE; SSIM is the mean over all frames. Per-frame logs are downloadable.
  • Speedup: BF16 execution seconds ÷ FP8 execution seconds. Above 1× is faster. The aggregate is the ratio of the five total times. The historical ratio uses only original comfy_execution_seconds, excluding queue, input-download and upload time.
  • Controls: every input file was hashed; production request settings and backend times were recovered with mlagent-uid-lookup. Fresh replays are direct local inference runs, so their UIDs and timings are in the linked process/result logs, not inserted into the production database.
  • Limits: single timing samples cannot establish statistical significance. Adapter-only FP8 says nothing about quantizing the base or other H3 configurations. These jobs use supplied driving audio, which is muxed unchanged; audio-generation quality is not tested.
